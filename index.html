🎨 How to use Kauvi 3D Studio

1
Add a shape
Click any shape in the left column — a box, sphere, cylinder. It pops into your scene.
2
Move it around
Click your shape, then drag the red/green/blue arrows to slide it. Drag the colored rings to rotate.
3
Paint it
Use the Color picker at the bottom (Model). Try the chips for quick colors!
4
Take a look around
Drag empty space to spin the view. Top/Front/Left etc. on the right jump to fixed angles.
5
Undo a mistake
Hit Undo (or Ctrl+Z). The 📜 History button shows everything you did.
6
Save your work
💾 Save keeps it in this browser. ⬇ Project downloads a file you can re-open later.
